# **Education AI Tutor & Class Assistant**

## **Product Requirements Document (PRD)**

**Product type:** AI-powered education and exam-preparation platform  
 **Primary market:** Nigeria  
 **Primary users:** Senior Secondary School students (SS1–SS3)  
 **Core examinations:** WAEC, NECO and JAMB/UTME  
 **Core promise:** Give Nigerian students access to an affordable, personalized AI tutor and learning assistant that helps them understand their school subjects, prepare for examinations and learn effectively with very little mobile data.

---

# **1\. Product Vision**

Create a digital learning platform that gives every Nigerian secondary-school student access to a **personal tutor, exam coach and class assistant**.

The platform should combine:

* AI tutoring

* WAEC, NECO and JAMB syllabus-based learning

* Real teacher lessons

* AI teaching videos

* Live AI and teacher Q\&A

* Practice questions

* Mock examinations

* Personalized learning plans

* Student study groups

* Progress tracking

* Voice and image-based learning

* Nigerian English and Nigerian Pidgin support

* Extremely low-data learning

The product should feel less like an online textbook and more like:

> **“A personal teacher that knows what I understand, what I don't understand, what I need to study next, and how to help me improve.”**

---

# **3\. Core Product Principles**

The product should follow these principles.

### **3.1 Learning before answering**

The AI should not simply provide answers.

Its objective is to help students **understand how to arrive at the answer**.

### **3.2 Every mistake is a learning opportunity**

When a student gets a question wrong, the system should determine an appropriate response.

Depending on the situation, it may:

* Give a hint

* Ask the student to try again

* Explain the mistake

* Re-teach the concept

* Give a simpler example

* Give additional practice

* Move the student to a more appropriate difficulty level

### **3.3 Personalized learning**

Every student should have an evolving learning profile based on:

* Topics studied

* Questions attempted

* Mistakes

* Strengths

* Weaknesses

* Assessment results

* Learning progress

* Exam goals

### **3.4 Low-data first**

Students should be able to learn extensively without depending on video.

The core learning experience should work effectively through:

* Text

* AI conversation

* Questions

* Answers

* Audio

* Low-data video

Video should enhance learning, not become a requirement.

### **3.5 Nigerian-first education**

Examples, explanations and communication should feel relevant to Nigerian students.

The product should support:

* Nigerian English

* Nigerian Pidgin

* Nigerian school context

* WAEC/NECO/JAMB preparation

* Local examples where appropriate

# **5\. Curriculum and Examination Experience**

Students should be able to select:

* WAEC

* NECO

* JAMB/UTME

* School examinations

They should then select their subjects.

The platform should organize learning according to the relevant approved curriculum/syllabus.

Students should have two major learning experiences.

## **Learn Mode**

The student follows the curriculum and learns topics progressively.

Example:

> Mathematics → Algebra → Simultaneous Equations → Lesson → Examples → Practice → Assessment

## **Exam Mode**

The student focuses on examination preparation.

Example:

> JAMB Mathematics → Algebra → Practice Questions → Timed Test → Results → Weakness Analysis → Revision Plan

The student should be able to switch between the two modes.

# **6\. Student Onboarding**

When a student first joins, the platform should ask simple questions to understand their goals.

### **Basic information**

* Class: SS1, SS2 or SS3

* Examination(s)

* Subjects

* Preferred learning style

* Preferred language

* Current academic confidence

* Intended university/course, if known

* Career interest, if known

### **Course and career guidance**

Students may tell the system what they want to study or become.

For example:

> Medicine  
>  Engineering  
>  Law  
>  Accounting  
>  Economics  
>  Architecture

The platform can explain relevant subject requirements and help students understand what they may need to focus on.

The student should always remain in control of their choices.

---

# **7\. Personalized Learning Plan**

After onboarding, the AI should create an initial learning plan.

The plan should consider:

* Examination date

* Class level

* Selected subjects

* Syllabus coverage

* Current performance

* Weak topics

* Strong topics

* Available study time

* Previous assessments

The plan should continuously change as the student improves.

For example:

> **Today's recommended learning**

> 1. Chemistry — Acids and Bases

> 2. 10 practice questions

> 3. Review yesterday's mistakes

> 4. 15-minute Mathematics revision

The student should be able to override the recommendation and choose another topic.

---

# **8\. AI Tutor**

The AI tutor is the central product experience.

It should behave like a patient personal teacher rather than a generic chatbot.

## **Teaching capabilities**

The tutor should be able to:

* Explain concepts

* Give examples

* Break complex subjects into simpler steps

* Ask students questions

* Give hints

* Correct mistakes

* Generate practice exercises

* Test understanding

* Summarize lessons

* Revise previous topics

* Compare concepts

* Give exam preparation advice

* Help students prepare for tests

* Recommend what to study next

## **Adaptive teaching**

The tutor should automatically adjust based on student performance.

For example:

A student struggles with quadratic equations.

The AI may:

1. Identify the misunderstanding.

2. Explain the relevant concept.

3. Give a simple example.

4. Ask a guided question.

5. Let the student attempt it.

6. Correct the mistake.

7. Give another question.

8. Increase difficulty when the student demonstrates understanding.

---

# **9\. Student-Controlled Teaching Styles**

Students should be able to tell the AI how they want to learn.

Examples:

> “Explain it like I'm a beginner.”

> “Give me more examples.”

> “Don't give me the answer. Give me a hint.”

> “Ask me questions instead.”

> “Make the questions harder.”

> “Explain this in Nigerian Pidgin.”

> “Teach me like I'm preparing for WAEC.”

> “Give me a short explanation.”

> “Explain it step-by-step.”

The AI should remember the student's preferred style but allow them to change it at any time.

---

# **10\. Language and Communication**

English should be the default academic language.

The platform should also prominently support **Nigerian Pidgin**.

Students should be able to switch between:

* Standard English

* Nigerian Pidgin

* A mixture of English and Pidgin where appropriate

Academic terminology should remain accurate even when an explanation is given in Pidgin.

The objective is clarity, not slang.

---

# **11\. Voice Learning**

Students should be able to communicate with the tutor using voice.

Possible experiences include:

> Student speaks a question → AI responds.

The student should also be able to listen to explanations rather than reading everything.

Voice should be especially useful for:

* English

* Literature

* Revision

* Explanations

* Students who prefer listening

* Students with limited typing ability

---

# **12\. Image-Based Question Help**

Students should be able to take or provide a picture of a question.

For example:

> Student photographs a Mathematics question.

They can ask:

> “Please teach me how to solve this. Don't just give me the answer.”

The AI should:

1. Understand the question.

2. Identify the subject/topic where possible.

3. Explain the method.

4. Guide the student through the solution.

5. Check understanding.

6. Provide a similar practice question.

This should work across appropriate subjects, including Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology and other subjects where questions can be presented visually.

---

# **13\. Video Learning**

Video should have two major sources.

## **Real Teacher Lessons**

Professional teachers provide deeper lessons and human explanations.

## **AI Teaching Videos**

AI-generated videos provide short, focused explanations.

AI videos should be especially useful for:

* Difficult concepts

* Quick revision

* Definitions

* Examples

* Exam tips

* Topic summaries

* Common mistakes

The platform should favor **short, focused videos** rather than making every lesson a long lecture.

---

# **14\. Low-Data Experience**

The product's low-data objective is a major differentiator.

A student should be able to learn without relying heavily on video.

The platform should prioritize:

1. Text learning

2. AI conversation

3. Practice questions

4. Audio

5. Low-data video

Students should have a visible **Data Saver** option.

Data Saver should favor:

* Text

* Lightweight lessons

* Lower-data audio

* Lower-data video

* Reduced visual content

* Minimal unnecessary media

There should be **no lesson/video downloading requirement or download feature** as part of the core experience.

The product goal is for students to achieve substantial learning within a very small data budget, with video treated as optional enrichment.

---

# **15\. Practice Questions**

Practice should be central to the platform.

Students should receive:

* Questions after lessons

* Topic-specific questions

* Difficulty-based questions

* Exam-style questions

* Adaptive questions

* Revision questions

* Mock examination questions

The AI should continuously adjust questions based on performance.

---

# **16\. Question Difficulty**

Questions should progressively move through levels such as:

* Beginner

* Basic

* Intermediate

* Advanced

* Examination level

The system should not simply make questions harder because a student gets several questions correct.

It should consider whether the student actually understands the concept.

---

# **17\. Learning From Wrong Answers**

When a student answers incorrectly, the platform should intelligently respond.

Possible responses:

### **Minor mistake**

Give a hint and allow another attempt.

### **Conceptual misunderstanding**

Explain the underlying concept.

### **Repeated mistake**

Return to an earlier prerequisite topic.

### **Careless error**

Point out the likely error and allow another attempt.

### **Persistent difficulty**

Create additional practice and add the topic to the student's revision plan.

This should make wrong answers useful rather than discouraging.

---

# **18\. Mock Examinations**

Students should have access to realistic examination experiences.

The platform should provide:

* Timed examinations

* Untimed examinations

* Subject-specific tests

* Topic tests

* Full mock examinations

* Revision tests

After completion, the AI should analyze the student's performance.

---

# **19\. AI Exam Analysis**

After an assessment, students should receive understandable feedback.

Example:

> **Mathematics Mock Exam**

> Score: 62%

> **Strong areas**

> * Algebra

> * Statistics

> **Needs attention**

> * Geometry

> * Trigonometry

> **AI recommendation**

> * Review Geometry

> * Complete 15 Trigonometry questions

> * Retake assessment

The system should then update the student's learning plan.

---

# **20\. Syllabus Progress**

Students should be able to see how much of their selected syllabus they have covered.

Example:

> **WAEC Biology**

> Completed: 64%

> Strong: 8 topics  
>  Developing: 5 topics  
>  Needs revision: 3 topics

Progress should represent meaningful learning rather than simply opening a lesson.

---

# **21\. Live Question & Answer**

The platform should support both:

### **AI Live Q\&A**

Students ask questions and receive immediate assistance.

### **Teacher Live Q\&A**

Students interact with real teachers.

### **Demand-Based Sessions**

When many students are struggling with the same topic, the platform can identify the demand and organize a relevant live session.

The system should not depend on a rigid recurring timetable as the only way to access live help.

---

# **22\. AI \+ Teacher Collaboration**

AI and teachers should have complementary roles.

### **AI**

Best for:

* Immediate answers

* Repeated explanations

* Personalized practice

* Individual revision

* Common questions

* Continuous tutoring

### **Human teachers**

Best for:

* Complex explanations

* Deeper classroom discussions

* Student motivation

* Nuanced questions

* Group learning

* Human interaction

The AI should not pretend to be a human teacher.

---

Students should be able to join educational study groups.

Examples:

* SS2 Mathematics Group

* WAEC Biology Revision Group

* JAMB Chemistry Group

* Literature Revision Group

Study groups can support:

* Discussions

* Questions

* Peer learning

* Collaborative revision

* Group challenges

The AI may participate as a **learning assistant**.

* AI-generated answers

* Teacher responses

* Student responses

The environment should remain educational rather than becoming a general social network.

* Recent mistakes

# **25\. AI General Assistance**

The tutor should be able to answer reasonable questions outside the immediate lesson.

However, education remains the primary purpose.

If a student asks:

> “What is inflation?”

The AI can explain it.

If the question is unrelated to learning, the AI can answer appropriately but should not allow general conversation to overwhelm the student's learning experience.

The product should remain an education platform, not a general-purpose social chatbot.

---

# **26\. Parent Experience**

A future parent area should allow parents/guardians to understand progress without invading the student's learning experience.

Parents could see:

* Subjects being studied

* Learning activity

* General progress

* Assessment results

* Areas requiring attention

* Exam preparation progress

The parent experience should emphasize **support rather than surveillance**.

---

# **27\. Teacher Experience**

Teachers should eventually have their own experience.

Teachers should be able to:

* Create or manage classes

* See student progress

* Assign practice

* Create assessments

* Monitor difficult topics

* Conduct live Q\&A

* Review student questions

* Identify common class weaknesses

* Provide explanations

* Support AI-assisted learning

The AI can help teachers identify:

> “Most students in this class are struggling with simultaneous equations.”

That information can help the teacher decide what to teach next.

---

# **28\. Class Assistant**

The AI should function as a class assistant as well as an individual tutor.

A teacher could ask:

> “Which topics did my class struggle with this week?”

The system could summarize:

* Common mistakes

* Difficult topics

* Student participation

* Assessment performance

* Areas needing revision

The teacher remains responsible for teaching decisions.

Useful motivation features include:

* Learning streaks

* Personal milestones

* Syllabus progress

* Topic mastery

* Practice achievements

* Revision goals

* Personal improvement

The product should emphasize:

> **“Improve your understanding.”**

rather than:

> **“Beat other students.”**

Notifications should be useful rather than annoying.

Examples:

> “You have 15 minutes of Mathematics revision planned today.”

> “You have improved your Chemistry score by 12%.”

> “You have 3 Biology topics that need revision.”

> “A live Chemistry Q\&A is available.”

Students should be able to control notification frequency.

# **31\. Accessibility and Simplicity**

The experience should be designed for students using affordable smartphones and varying levels of digital experience.

The product should prioritize:

* Simple navigation

* Clear language

* Large readable content

* Minimal unnecessary visual complexity

* Low-data operation

* Voice interaction

* Nigerian Pidgin support

* Easy access to the AI tutor

Students should not need extensive training to understand the platform.

---

# **32\. Trust and Educational Accuracy**

Because students will use the platform for important examinations, accuracy is critical.

The platform should distinguish between:

* Official curriculum information

* Approved educational material

* Original teaching content

* AI-generated explanations

* Student-generated content

Where appropriate, the product should encourage students to verify important information against official examination guidance.

The AI should not confidently invent syllabus requirements, examination rules or answers.

---

# **33\. Copyright and Educational Content**

The product should use educational materials appropriately and ensure that content used from WAEC, NECO, JAMB or other sources is properly authorized where required.

The platform should invest heavily in:

* Original explanations

* Original examples

* Original practice questions

* Properly licensed materials

* Teacher-created lessons

* Properly authorized examination content

---

# **34\. Safety and Student Protection**

Because the primary users are minors, student protection should be a fundamental product requirement.

The platform should include appropriate safeguards around:

* Student communication

* Study groups

* Teacher interaction

* Live sessions

* User-generated content

* Privacy

* Inappropriate content

* Bullying

* Harassment

* Attempts to exploit students

The platform should create an environment that is clearly educational and appropriate for secondary-school students.

# **39\. Product Success Measures**

The most important measure should not simply be the number of students registered.

The product should measure whether students are actually learning.

Important indicators include:

* Students returning to study

* Lessons completed

* Topics mastered

* Improvement between assessments

* Reduction in repeated mistakes

* Syllabus coverage

* Practice completion

* Mock-exam improvement

* Student engagement with the AI tutor

* Student satisfaction

* Teacher participation

* Live Q\&A participation

* Data consumed per learning session

* Students achieving their learning goals

The ultimate question is:

> **Are students becoming better at understanding and answering questions?**

The product is an **AI-powered Nigerian education platform for SS1–SS3 students** that combines syllabus-based learning, personalized tutoring, examination preparation, real teachers, AI teaching videos, live Q\&A, practice, mock examinations and study groups.

The AI continuously learns what each student knows and does not know, then adapts the student's learning journey accordingly.
